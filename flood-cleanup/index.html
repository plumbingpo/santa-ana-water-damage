Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Santa Ana Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across Santa Ana and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

Cleanup opens with rapid extraction: truck-mounted units and submersible pumps that pull thousands of gallons an hour out of the building. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Consider flood insurance even if you are not in a designated flood zone
  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
