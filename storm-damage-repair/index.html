Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding

Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
